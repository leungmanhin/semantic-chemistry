# Event Narratives

Dated episodes of two years in Aelmere, written in the controlled language of `style_guide.md` (EPISODIC register: one happening per sentence, past tense, named or definite participants, the bounded connective cues because / so / as a result, sealing only where a report's content is the point). Days count from the Year-1 autumn equinox (day 0); Year 2 begins on day 365. Each paragraph is ONE EPISODE and is parsed as ONE PASSAGE, so a definite refers back within the episode; the episodes give the world rules their concrete instances. Lines beginning with `>` are design annotations and are not part of the parseable corpus; the machine-facing parse input is `events.json`.

## Part X. Spring of Year 2

On day 540 of Year 2, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch.
Sailsworn reported to the Council that the salt-bloom store held twelve weeks
of supply. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the feather store held five
weeks of supply. The Council continued the doubled feather ration at the
Stilllight Station through the spring. The Council asked the apprentices to
collect feathers more thoroughly in the molting season. Hesper reported to the
Council that the mire-essence reserve held eight months of supply. Hesper said
that a strong summer thread harvest would restore the reserve.

On day 555 of Year 2, the spring equinox came. The day was clear and warm. The
Council closed the salt-bloom season at the next full-moon meeting. The
stations of the Cliff Path stepped down salt-bloom additions over the
following two weeks. The Northcove returned to its summer cadence. Cauldron
Hall increased its distillation. Hesper assigned an extra apprentice to the
morning collection of silken threads.

On day 575 of Year 2, the cliff-spires were clearly visible from Aelmere in
the morning. Keepers walked to the base of the cliff. The keepers counted the
sky-cats on each cliff-spire. The keepers counted fourteen sky-cats. The
estimate of the previous year was twelve sky-cats. The Council logged the
census as an estimate.

On day 590 of Year 2, Coraline Ash told Eli that salt-bloom slows the burn of
mire-essence. Coraline Ash told Eli that the cliff-spires shield the Northcove
against the north wind. Coraline Ash told Eli that the lanterns of the
Northcove need no salt-bloom. Coraline Ash told Eli that a lantern burning at
full rate produces more mist-light. Eli wrote the answers in her notebook.
