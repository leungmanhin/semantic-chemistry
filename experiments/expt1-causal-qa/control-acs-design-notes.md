# Control-level closure — design rationale (V3-B)

Why the control ACS is designed exactly the way it is, and the rejected designs that
shaped it. The build spec lives with the implementation; this file records the *reasons*,
so the choices aren't re-litigated (or quietly re-introduced) later.

## The goal

Ben's ACS definition (`docs/ACS_notes_16-7-2026.txt`) makes an ACS three separate things:
**structurally closed** (every non-food semantic or control motif needed to sustain its
reactions is regenerated by the set itself — endogenously; substrate, control, or hybrid),
**metabolically viable** (earnings cover firing costs, rent, decay), and **causally
useful** (ablation measurably changes downstream utility, not reproduced by redundant
alternatives). External reward is **food** — it sustains and selects, but never closes.

Classic V3 fails closure honestly: its cue-converters are feed-forward (surface cues are
food), so it is a viable + useful *subsidized candidate*, not an ACS. Ben: in small QA
examples "closure may be more naturally visible at the control level" — the semantic
content flows question→answer and never reconstructs the question, but a *reusable
inference-control pattern* can recur across questions. V3-B builds exactly that loop.

## The design

```
R_detect:    WhyQuestion(q) → Attempting(q, cause-strat)   sm 1   routing      [outside ACS]
R_explore:   Attempting     → Primed(cause-strat)          ex 1   spin-up      [outside ACS]
R_so_rf:     Primed ∧ So        → ReasonFor                sm 1   substrate    [member]
R_result_rf: Primed ∧ AsAResult → ReasonFor                sm 1   substrate    [member]
R_trans_rf:  Primed ∧ RF ∧ RF   → ReasonFor                sm 1 + tr 1        [member]
R_worked:    ReasonFor → Worked                            sm 1   bridge       [member]
R_learn:     Worked    → Habit      (persists)             sm 1   consolidate  [member]
R_prime:     Habit     → Primed                            sm 1   re-spin-up   [member]
```

- `Primed` (ready this cycle) and `Worked` (a derivation just succeeded) are working
  state — they decay every cycle. `Habit` (the strategy is established) is long-term —
  it survives decay via a declared persistence exemption.
- `ex` is a finite **birth endowment no clamp mints** — the exploration budget.
- The ring `Primed → substrate → ReasonFor → Worked → Habit → Primed` is the ACS.
  `ReasonFor` sits on the cycle, so the closure kind is **hybrid** (substrate + control),
  which Ben explicitly allows; a "pure control" label would need an artificial side-output
  arrangement and would misdescribe the loop.

Life course: questions ignite the strategy through `R_detect`/`R_explore` while `ex`
lasts; `R_worked`/`R_learn` consolidate the `Habit`; once `ex` exhausts, the fuel gate
silences `R_explore` and the loop's `R_prime` is the strategy's only life-support,
funded by minted income. Ablate the loop and the system dies when its exploration
endowment runs out — the mortal-computation starvation signature, now at the control level.

## Design principles (what every rejected variant violated)

1. **Nothing external closes the loop.** Evaluator reward, harness seeds, and per-task
   stipends are food. Closure = the set regenerating its own non-food ingredients.
2. **The loop must pump state across the decay boundary through a persistent store.**
   A "self-loop" that only closes within a cycle is a monotonic no-op; recurrence across
   cycles is what closure means operationally here. This is also the subset-miner's
   self-sufficiency test, and it is **count-agnostic** — member size (1 vs many) is not the
   criterion. A component certifies iff its regenerating cycle carries at least one
   persistent motif that a member produces, so it re-fires across decay from its own
   products rather than being re-ignited from food. `{R_trans}` in classic V3 fails not for
   being a singleton, nor for consuming two `ReasonFor` and producing one (the engine is
   monotonic — nothing is depleted), but because its whole cycle is transient `ReasonFor`:
   every turn decay wipes it and the converters re-supply base edges from food. Genuine
   self-maintenance requires a decay-surviving anchor the loop itself regenerates (`Habit`).
3. **The pump must be causally load-bearing and non-redundant.** Ablating it must
   measurably reduce downstream utility (reward/surplus, with answer loss as the visible
   corollary), and no always-on alternative path may fully reproduce its contribution.
4. **Ignition is task-driven; sustainment is endogenous.** The environment may spark the
   strategy (food + endowment); only the loop may keep it alive.
5. **Certification is regime-relative.** Like the clamp-switch for surplus, whether the
   loop certifies depends on the economy it lives in (endowment-once vs perpetually
   re-subsidized). This is a feature of mortal semantics, not an artifact.

## Rejected designs, and why

### 1. `(seed c1)` — a runner-injected `Primed` spark
One `Primed` atom placed by the harness before cycle 1 (with a decaying id, so the loop
had to take over from cycle 2). **Rejected:** the harness would have to magically know
*which* strategy (among many, in production) solves the task in hand — a control atom
with no producer story. Ignition belongs to the task itself: the question's `WhyQuestion`
edge fires `R_detect`, and the spark's *resource* is the organism's own `ex` endowment —
which is a standard, already-modeled channel (birth capital), not a bespoke magic atom.

### 2. `R_detect` primes directly (cheap, every question)
`WhyQuestion → Primed` at negligible cost, every cycle. The ring still *structurally*
closes (the edges exist), but its contribution is fully reproduced by the food path —
each question re-supplies `Primed` for free, so ablating `R_prime` (or `R_worked`, or
`R_learn`) changes nothing. **Rejected** by Ben's redundancy clause: the loop's
contribution must "not be entirely reproduced by redundant alternatives."
Two lessons this variant taught:
- A naive condition-4 (whole-set ablation judged on answer count) would *spuriously
  certify* it — suppressing the whole set kills the substrate members and answers with
  them. Whole-set answer-count ablation is blind to intra-set redundancy. This is what
  motivated upgrading condition 4 to multi-cycle replay judged on reward/surplus delta
  (the doc's own judgment: "reduce task performance, explanation quality, novelty, or
  some other relevant outcome"; the schema's `acs-do-influence` is a minted-reward drop).
- Without `R_prime` at all, the chain dead-ends at `Habit` (nothing consumes it): no
  cycle, no closure, correctly no ACS. `R_prime` is *the* closing edge.

### 3. Base-vs-advanced split (chaining as a "practiced skill")
One-hop rules cold-startable, `R_trans` alone gated on `Primed`, making the loop
load-bearing only for multi-hop answers. **Rejected:** it conflates *the strategy* with
one skill (transitivity). A causal-QA strategy must be formable from one-hop successes
too — there is no principled reason a strategy exists only when composition is needed.
The split was engineering-for-closure, not modeling.

### 4. Folding the control clauses into the substrate rules' RHS
Attach the loop's products directly, e.g. `R_so_rf: Primed ∧ So → ReasonFor ∧ Primed`.
Three sub-cases:
- **Transient `Primed` on the RHS:** cannot pump across the decay boundary. At cycle-2
  start no `Primed` exists, the rule needs it to fire, and only firing produces it —
  deadlock. The self-loop closes only within a cycle, where (in a monotonic engine) it
  is a no-op.
- **Persistent `Primed` on the RHS:** a write-once flag. After ignition the
  "regeneration" only re-adds an atom that is already there; ablating it changes nothing,
  ever — inert under condition 4.
- **Folded `Habit` (keep `R_prime`):** actually legitimate — same organism, fewer
  organs — and it would certify. The factored chain is kept anyway because: the control
  rules are *strategy-generic* (written once, reused across strategies; the wrapper adds
  one premise to a domain rule instead of rewriting its RHS, keeping mined domain rules
  pure); SC §7.1's expected QA ACS is a *list of distinct processing members*; and
  separate members give leave-one-out ablation something to test.

The general worry behind this variant — "a strategy-wrapping pass could certify *any*
DAG" — resolves as: wrapping buys only *structural* closure, which is cheap by design.
Certification is the conjunction: the wrapped system must also survive on the loop after
the endowment exhausts (viability), die under loop-ablation (usefulness), and not be
re-suppliable by a redundant food path. A wrapping with all three properties *is* real
self-maintenance — it cannot be faked without being made real. Economics disciplines the
rest: every control rule pays firing costs each cycle (rehearsal is a real tax), so
indiscriminate wrapping is metabolically dominated by wrapping only what pays.

### 5. Per-task fuel re-endowment (fresh `ex` per question/chamber)
Modeling each new question as a fresh chamber + fresh fuel snapshot (with new `ex`).
**Rejected** on both counts:
- **Doc-unfaithful.** Chambers are *standing, context-typed* environments — the causal-QA
  chamber processes the whole stream of its question type (MSC §7.1: reward "rule genomes
  that repeatedly solve variants of the same question type"; MSC §5.1: chambers are
  components of the persistent state, run as update operators; SC §4.4: chambers are
  context conjunctions that "tend to form different ACSs"). What a worker run receives
  per invocation is a *snapshot* — a replay/provisioning device (MSC §6.2), never a fuel
  grant. Fuel is organism-owned (`(fuel O tok n)`, MSC §4.1/§6.1); its only channels are
  birth endowment and evaluator minting. New questions arrive as **food** (molecules +
  `WhyQuestion` edge) into the standing chamber; the resident organism spends and earns
  from its own purse across the stream.
- **It un-certifies the loop.** Perpetually re-seeded `ex` makes exploration forever
  affordable, so the habit loop is redundant again (variant 2's failure, resurfaced at
  the harness level) — and the loop then *correctly* fails certification. Whether the
  control ACS is an ACS depends on the economy regime; the honest regime is
  endowment-once, earn-thereafter.

The organism-lifecycle corollary: the expressed soma *resides* in its chamber, reacting
to each arriving question until starvation — there is no per-task quote/dequote cycle.
Genome re-expression (with its gated dequote cost and parent-funded endowment) is the
*reproduction/colonization* path — how a certified strategy revives after death or
enters another chamber — not the routine task path.

## Why the remaining pieces are the way they are

- **`ex` is the doc's TECAN *welfare* flow** (TECAN §4.6 "Welfare = exploration endowment,
  supports uncertain high-upside work"; §2 "supplies welfare to preserve exploration";
  §5.1 epoch step 24 `ApplyRentTaxWelfare`; §7.4 welfare for "high expected future causal
  value"). Its *source is the system's economic regulation*, not task-reward minting
  (that's Wage) and not inheritance — so it needs no living parent, which is why a new
  exploratory attempt after a die-off (e.g. a clamp switch) is welfare-funded regardless of
  lineage. It is distinct from *offspring endowment* `e` (MSC §5.3, parent→child from earned
  surplus at reproduction) — do not conflate them; `ex` is welfare, not inheritance.
  Welfare is *conditioned* (uncertain + high-expected-value), so it **withdraws as a strategy
  consolidates** — that withdrawal, not a fixed budget, is what drives the exploration→habit
  transition and keeps the habit loop load-bearing (an *unconditional* per-epoch `ex` would
  re-open the redundancy cheat; a conditioned/tapering one does not). Our finite `ex` seeded
  at genesis is the **degenerate binary stand-in** for conditioned per-epoch welfare — a
  budget that *exhausts* crudely approximates welfare that *withdraws* — exactly parallel to
  the `Habit` persistence exemption standing in for importance-graded forgetting. The
  faithful mechanism (per-epoch, expected-value-allocated welfare; rent as its mirror) is the
  deferred TECAN economy. Ablation signature is preserved: without the loop, death arrives
  once the (stand-in) exploration support is gone.
- **`Habit` persistence exemption:** decay models working-memory forgetting; a habit is
  long-term memory. Doc-faithfully, forgetting is importance-graded (TECAN), and control
  motifs are molecules in the chamber's store (MSC §2.1) — a binary `persistent`
  declaration is the degenerate stand-in until graded attention exists. `Habit` is
  chamber-scoped strategy memory maintained by the resident organism.
- **`R_worked` fires on the derivation, never on the evaluator's verdict:** the loop must
  close *without* external reward (principle 1). Success here = "a `ReasonFor` was
  produced," an endogenous event. Minting stays what it is — viability, not closure.
- **`R_detect`/`R_explore` outside the ACS:** they consume food (`WhyQuestion`,
  `Attempting` rooted in food) and nothing in the set regenerates their inputs — they are
  the intake, structurally feed-forward. That they are *causally* necessary (ablate
  `R_detect` → nothing ever ignites) while *structurally* outside the loop is expected:
  membership-on-the-cycle and causal necessity are different questions, and the
  certification stack asks both separately.
